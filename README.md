Credivaro is an open-source Android app for intelligent debt management, loan tracking, credit cards, installments, smart reminders, and repayment optimization.

## Project status

Planning and design. No installable application or APK is available yet.

## Planned features
- Manage loans, credit cards and installments with custom lender terms.
- Track confirmed balances, scheduled and actual payments.
- Generate a financial calendar with payment, statement, grace-period and limit-reset events.
- Configure flexible reminders and privacy-sensitive notification content.
- Forecast expenses and compare repayment scenarios with explicit assumptions.
- Work offline without compulsory accounts or bank integrations.

## Principles
Local-first, transparent calculations, accessibility, privacy by default, and Russian/English localization. Credivaro is not a lender and does not execute payments. Forecasts do not replace bank statements or professional advice.

## Development
Planned stack: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Room, DataStore, WorkManager and AlarmManager where appropriate. Build instructions will be added after Android initialization.
